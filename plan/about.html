About & privacy

This planner is run by ai901 in Memphis. It is a free, any-store grocery budget helper for families.

Privacy

No account. We do not collect your plans, pantry picks, or receipts. Receipt tools run only on your phone or computer. Your receipts stay on your phone. Delete anytime. Waitlist signup uses your own email app — we only see what you choose to send.

Prices

Average U.S. prices, week of Nov 2, 2026. Shelf price always wins. Totals are planning estimates, not a promise of what you will pay.

Allergies & health

Meal ideas only. Always check labels for allergens. This tool is not nutrition advice and not medical advice.
