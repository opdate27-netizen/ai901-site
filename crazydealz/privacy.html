Crazy Dealz Tires privacy note

Last updated November 15, 2026. Online sign-up on this website is not open yet. This note covers contact information you share with the shop by calling or at the counter.

What we collect

If you give the shop your name, phone number, email address, or vehicle details so we can help you, we keep that information for shop use. We do not collect sign-ups through a form on this website today.

Why we collect it

To reach you about your visit, a quote, or service reminders you asked for. We do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

Texts

Texting is not set up yet. Sharing your phone number with the shop does not sign you up for marketing texts.

We do not sell your information

Crazy Dealz Tires does not sell, rent or trade your information. It is kept in the shop's private customer records, only for the reasons above. ai901, the shop's website and marketing helper, may help the shop under the same rules.

How to reach us or ask us to stop

Call (646) 506-8189 to ask us to correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. We will handle that request within 10 business days.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we need it for your service or while you have asked us to stay in touch. You can ask us to delete or correct it at any time by calling the shop.

Kids

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
