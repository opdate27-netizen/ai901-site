Three steps. No tech headaches.

STEP 03

Before your Home Base goes live, we will:

Build on established platforms

We will build your system on GoHighLevel, Retell AI, and Make. Each vendor reports an independent audit of its security controls, and their reports are available from the vendors on request.

Use platforms with encrypted storage

We will store your data in GoHighLevel and Make, which state that they encrypt stored data and data in transit.

Lock down logins

We will use two-factor authentication and a password manager for every account we touch. Your staff will only get the access they need.

Keep less data, for less time

We will set call recordings and transcripts to delete automatically after a period you choose, instead of keeping them forever.

Limit the AI to what you approve

Your assistant will answer from information you sign off on. We will instruct it so that if it doesn’t know a price or policy, it takes a message for your team instead of guessing.

Tell callers what’s happening

Calls answered by your AI assistant will open with a short notice that the call may be recorded and is handled by an AI assistant.

Text by the rules

We will register your business for carrier-approved texting (A2P 10DLC), add opt-in language to your forms, and honor opt-outs automatically.

Tell you if something goes wrong

We will have a written plan ready to shut off the affected piece, tell you quickly, and fix the cause.

Keep health info out by default

For healthcare offices, we will keep AI to booking and general questions and keep patient health information out of it. Handling health information needs signed agreements and paid upgrades, and we’ll walk you through it first.

Security audits and certifications belong to the vendors named above, not to ai901.help.

06 · FAQ

Straight answers.

What is Home Base?

One screen for your business, set up for you from established tools: missed calls caught, bookings and your morning brief. If your plan includes an AI phone line, its calls show up there too. The demo uses sample data; yours shows your own.

What does the pilot cost in total?

$297 for setup, plus a one-time texting registration fee (about $23, paid by you to the phone carriers, not refundable). You also pay the monthly carrier texting fees directly; we don’t cover them. No monthly fee from us during the 14-day pilot. After day 14, you either move to One Fix at $597 setup + $249/mo, or stop.

When do the 14 days start?

At go-live, not when you pay. Carrier text approval can take 10–15 days, and the clock starts only once your texting is approved and live. If it isn’t working by day 14 after go-live, I’ll fix it or refund your $297. (The one-time ~$23 carrier registration is paid by you to the phone carriers and isn’t refundable. You also pay the monthly carrier fees.) Before go-live, we agree in writing what “working” means, for example missed calls get a text back and your booking link works.

Do you build your own AI?

No. We set up established tools, not our own AI: established platforms like GoHighLevel and Retell AI. The custom part is the setup: choosing the right tools, configuring them with your information, connecting them, and teaching you to use them.

Which tools see my customers’ information?

The platforms we will set up for you, such as GoHighLevel (contacts, texts, calls, bookings), Retell AI (calls, if you use an AI phone line) and Make (only data passing through an automation, with copies kept in its logs for up to 30 days). We will list exactly which tools your setup uses before you start.

How long will my data be kept?

Your setup will delete call recordings and transcripts automatically after a period you choose; we will set that before go-live. Each vendor also has its own timelines after an account is closed, and we’ll walk you through them.

Is it safe for medical or dental offices?

By default, your setup will keep AI to booking and general questions and keep patient health information out of it. Handling health information needs signed agreements and paid upgrades. Ask before we start.

Will the AI make things up?

Your assistant will be set up to answer only from information you sign off on. AI can still make mistakes, so we will set limits together and review it with you.
