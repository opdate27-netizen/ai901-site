What Happens to the Calls You Can't Answer?

Quick take: play the game, then see how a missed call can turn into a text with a booking link.

It’s 7pm. You’re flat on your back under a sink, hands full. Your phone rings. You can’t grab it.

By the time you call back, they may have already called the next plumber.

You can’t answer every call. But a missed call doesn’t have to be a dead end.

Same idea, pointed at a boring problem that costs you money: missed calls.

How missed-call text-back works

  1. You miss the call. You’re under the sink.
  2. They get a text right away, with a link to your booking calendar.
  3. They can book a time while you finish the job.

It’s not magic. It needs your real hours and info, clear limits, and a person checking that it works. That’s the part I do.
